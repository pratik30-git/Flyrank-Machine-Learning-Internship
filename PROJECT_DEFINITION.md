# Search Engagement Opportunity Scoring System

## 2. Define the Actual Decision

### Question
Which pages should the SEO team prioritize for potential search-engagement improvement?

### Decision
Decide which pages should be reviewed first based on their search engagement opportunity.

### Action
The SEO team investigates the prioritized pages and considers potential improvements to titles, descriptions, content, or search-intent alignment.

### Cost of a Wrong Call
The team could waste time reviewing pages with little opportunity, while pages with stronger opportunities could be overlooked.
